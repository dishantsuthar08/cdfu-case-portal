RE: Assignment — Solvex Analytics wire fraud incident

You are assigned to Case CDF-2026-0001, opened following a fraud complaint filed by Solvex Analytics Pvt. Ltd., a data-analytics firm of roughly 140 employees. On the date of complaint, Solvex Finance confirmed that a scheduled vendor payment of $86,400 was routed to an account that did not belong to the intended recipient, Meridian Systems Pvt. Ltd. Approximately 18% of the funds have since been recovered by the receiving bank's fraud team; the remainder is presumed unrecoverable pending this investigation.

This folder contains all evidence collected to date: photographs, printed communications, device evidence, access logs, and one handwritten witness statement. Unit 7's current caseload does not allow for a second pass through the physical evidence, so treat every item in this folder as final — nothing further will be collected after this point.

Your task is not to write a report. Your task is to determine, using only what is in this folder:

  • How did the attacker gain access to Solvex's internal systems?
  • How did the attacker know enough real internal detail to make the fraud convincing?
  • Was any Solvex employee knowingly complicit — or only negligent?
  • What is the exact technical sequence that allowed the transfer to occur?

Do not contact Solvex Analytics directly. Do not request additional evidence. Work the folder as it stands.
