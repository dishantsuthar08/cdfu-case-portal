Statement of Complainant

I, Ananya Kapoor, am reporting that a scheduled payment to one of our regular vendors was redirected to a bank account that does not match the vendor's verified details on file. When I went back through the approval history for this transaction, it did not follow our normal authorization pattern — the record shows the bank details were updated shortly before the payment went out, without the usual verification step.
